# Benefit-first hooks and honest retention

Apply this to every new social video, especially videos for «تقني». Treat the times below as editing defaults to
test, not rules of TikTok's algorithm. User instructions and the chosen format still control voice and duration.

## Build the opening before the rest of the script
1. Write three distinct hook candidates: a concrete benefit, a recognizable problem, and a specific curiosity gap.
   Choose one that the footage and verified facts can deliver; adapt it to this topic rather than repeating a slogan.
2. Put the strongest useful benefit in the first spoken sentence and first 2–3 seconds. The viewer should already
   know what they gain. If free code is the main verified offer, say it here; do not save it for the final third.
3. Show the result or a meaningful action from frame zero and provide clear visual proof within roughly 3–5 seconds.
   Pair the spoken hook with the actual outcome, real product UI or source evidence.
4. Give a brief, specific reason to continue: «شوف كيف تخدم…» or
   «وفي الآخر نوريك [خطوة مفيدة محددة موجودة فعلاً في الفيديو]». Name the payoff rather than asking for attention.
5. Move directly into the demonstration or story. Put the tool name, branding explanation and any necessary recap
   after the benefit. Make each subsequent beat answer the question raised by the previous one.
6. End by delivering the promised step, result or answer and a practical takeaway. Keep useful information throughout;
   an extra final detail can reward completion without withholding the main benefit until the end.

## Keep the promise honest
- Do not use «استنى للنهاية»، «مفاجأة خطيرة» or repeated reminders without a concrete, fulfilled payoff.
- Do not imply free hosting, an open-source license, easy profit or guaranteed outcomes unless verified.
- Do not advertise a code link, download or final example that the delivered video does not actually supply.
- Keep spoken encouragement natural in Libyan Arabic. A reason to finish is not a request to follow the account.
- For silent videos, communicate the hook and reason to continue with readable on-screen text and visual progression.
  For logo stings and wordless loops, use a visual hook and resolution; do not add unsolicited narration or padding.
- For mysteries and news, establish the stakes and evidence early, then earn the explanation; do not invent suspense.

## Example: a restaurant app with an authorized free-code offer
Opening: «كود تطبيق مجاني لمطعمك… ضغطة منك، وتليفون الزبون ينبّهه إن طلبه جاهز. شوف كيف!»
Show «جاهز» being pressed and the real alert appearing during the opening; then demonstrate the customer steps.
Close with the promised usable example or practical next step. Do not spend twenty seconds before revealing the offer.

## Check the script and the actual opening frames
- Can a new viewer identify the benefit by second 3, with and without sound?
- Does the first visual prove or illustrate that benefit rather than merely decorate the words?
- Is the reason to finish specific, and is its payoff visibly delivered before the video ends?
- Would removing an introductory sentence bring useful information forward? Remove it if so.
- Keep the opening readable on a phone; do not fit a paragraph into a two-second shot.
- When analytics exist, compare retention, saves and attributed followers with similar-age results.
  Treat a suspected weak opening as a testable hypothesis, not a proven cause or guaranteed growth fix.
