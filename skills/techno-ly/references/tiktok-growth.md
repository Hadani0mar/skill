## Posting rules
- **One video per day, evenings 21:00–22:00 Tripoli** (best results). Never two the same evening — the new one
  splits the small test audience of the last one.
- A video that "slows" after 3–4 h has finished its first wave — don't delete/repost; waves come later.
- Order: value content first; service/promo videos after a few value videos (trust first).
- Series: open each video with its own concrete benefit and early result. Put any useful previous-video recap
  after the opening. Give a specific reason to finish and fulfil it in this video; a later episode never replaces
  the current payoff. Follow `hooks-and-retention.md` rather than older recap-first examples.

## On each post
- Caption: one-line benefit + the tool names + a question; 5 hashtags max (TikTok limit). Used:
  `#ذكاء_اصطناعي #ai #أدوات_ذكاء_اصطناعي #تقنية #تعلم` (AI lists), `#supabase #برمجة #مبرمجين #تطوير_تطبيقات
  #تعلم_البرمجة` (dev), `#متجر_الكتروني #ليبيا #تصميم_مواقع #موشن_جرافيك #تسويق_الكتروني` (store promo).
- First comment by the account, pinned: «اكتبلي اسم الأداة اللي تبي شرحها الكامل 👇 الأكثر طلب ينزل شرحه أول».
- Reply to every comment in the first hour; answer good questions with "reply with video".
- After 24 h read retention (average watch time, drop-off second) and fix the next hook.

## Profile
- Avatar: the ت mark (green version reads best small; test at 32–96 px on white/black/grey).
- Bio (≤80 chars): «نبسّطلك البرمجة في أقل من دقيقة ⚡ أدوات • شروحات • أخبار تقنية».
- Pin the best value video (or the channel intro once it's strong).
