# The storyboard page

One HTML file, fully self-contained: every image and font inlined as base64 (a page that loads files from a folder
shows up blank on someone else's computer; test it by opening a copy from an empty folder).

- One card per beat: a small animated frame (216x384, CSS keyframes on the real screens and real product photos)
  on the left, and on the right: what happens, the camera, the cut, the sound, and the time range.
- Beats: opening on the home screen · the question into the bar · typing · the dive · results, tap, lift ·
  into the bag (wide, then a closer cut at the mouth) · the next items · the basket rounds · the pour ·
  the lift onto the final screen · the cut · the closing line with the handle.
- A strip of the real product cut-outs, so the user approves the photos too.
- A sound section in plain words: which real sounds, and the banned list.
- At the end, the open decisions as clear options, with your recommendation first.

Wait for an explicit OK. Apply every note to the storyboard, not to a promise.
