# Sabha film — music prompt for Gemini (instrumental, 48 s)

Instrumental cinematic score, 48 seconds, no vocals, no lyrics. A Saharan Libyan city rising from ruin and division to unity. One continuous arc in five parts. Tempo rises from 68 to 104 BPM. Key: D Hijaz-flavoured minor at the start, resolving to D major at the end.

0:00-0:04 Near silence. Desert wind, a low sustained drone, one distant frame-drum pulse like a heartbeat at 68 BPM.
0:04-0:10 Solo oud, slow and warm, a sparse plucked melody in D Hijaz. A breathy ney joins at 0:08. Golden, nostalgic, spacious.
0:10-0:17 Ruin. The oud drops out. Low cellos and double basses with dark tremolo, one dissonant sustained note, sparse deep drum hits, a quiet metallic rumble. Tense but restrained, no loud explosions.
0:17-0:23 Division. Three separate solo instrument lines enter one by one in different registers (ney, solo cello, soft plucked oud). They play unrelated lines that do not resolve, wide stereo separation, no rhythm.
0:23-0:31 Rebuilding. A steady bendir and darbuka groove starts at 84 BPM and builds. Plucked oud ostinato, low strings in rhythmic eighth notes, a melody emerging in a major-leaning mode. Hopeful, rising energy.
0:31-0:36 Momentum at 96 BPM. Add deep frame drums and a string ostinato, lift toward major.
0:36-0:41 Unity. All instruments play the same melody together in unison and octaves. Full string swell with powerful frame drums, bright and proud. Modulate to D major.
0:41-0:48 Greatness despite constraints. A long sustained major chord with soaring strings and a single ney line above. The drums fade to a heartbeat, then a final resolved chord with a natural 2-second reverb tail. End cleanly at 0:48.

Mix: keep 300-3000 Hz fairly open for a voice-over, wide dynamics, no clipping, no abrupt cuts.
Avoid: pop beats, EDM, trap hi-hats, choir "ahh", Hollywood trailer braams, synth leads, Western cliches.
