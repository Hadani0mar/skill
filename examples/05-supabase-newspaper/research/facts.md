# Supabase — fact sheet (verified 2026-10-30)

- Founded January 2020 by Paul Copplestone (CEO) and Ant Wilson (CTO). — https://research.contrary.com/report/supabase
- Copplestone, previously CTO of Nimbus for Work, migrated it from Firebase to Postgres and found Postgres lacked real-time. — https://research.contrary.com/company/supabase
- By April 2020 Supabase hosted only eight databases. — https://research.contrary.com/company/supabase
- May 2020: tagline changed from "real-time Postgres" to "the open-source Firebase alternative"; eight → eight hundred hosted databases within three days. — https://research.contrary.com/report/supabase · https://www.basedash.com/blog/how-supabase-became-this-generation-s-database
- HN post "an open source Firebase alternative" got 1.1K+ upvotes. — https://research.contrary.com/company/supabase
- Paul: "we changed the tagline on the website one day" (… "and it went straight to the top of Hacker News"). — https://changelog.com/podcast/476
- Y Combinator Summer 2020 (S20). — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/supabase
- June 2026: $500M Series F at $10.5B post-money, led by GIC. — https://www.orrick.com/en/News/2026/06/Supabase-Raises-500-Million-at-10-5-Billion-Valuation-to-Accelerate-Lead-in-Agentic-Infrastructure
- 9M+ developers use Supabase. — same Orrick release
- Database launches +600% YoY; 60%+ of new databases launched by AI tools. — https://sacra.com/c/supabase/

Lesson: "real-time Postgres" said what it is; "the open-source Firebase alternative" said what it is for.
